How a job goes.

  1. 1

    Walkthrough

    We look at the room, confirm the colour and give you a price in writing.

  2. 2

    Prep

    Furniture moved and covered, holes patched, edges taped.

    Paint

    Two coats, with time to dry in between.

  4. 4

    Clean-up

    We tidy up and walk the room with you before we leave.

Questions.

Do you move the furniture?

Yes. We move it to the middle of the room and cover it. If something is heavy or fragile, tell us first.

How long does a room take?

Most rooms take one to two days, including time for each coat to dry.

Do you supply the paint?

We can bring the paint, or use yours. We agree which one in the quote.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Call, or text a photo, and we send a price in writing. No charge and no pressure.

Do you work in my area?

We paint in Dartmouth and Halifax. Not sure? Text us your postal code.

Ready for a free quote?

Call us, or text a photo of your room. We reply with a clear price.
